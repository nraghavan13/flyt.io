03 — Approach

How we think about a deal.

  1. Evidence over narrative

    A good story is a starting point. We look for the data, technology and customer proof underneath it.

  2. Selective by design

    We take on a small number of engagements so each gets senior attention end to end.

  3. Aligned and transparent

    Clear scope, clear fees and disclosed conflicts. We work for the investor.
